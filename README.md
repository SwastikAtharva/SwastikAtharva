## 🏆 Smart India Hackathon 2025 – Winning Project

### 📌 Unified Education Interface  
**Problem Statement ID:** 25252  

> 🥇 **Winner – Smart India Hackathon 2025 (Grand Finale)**  
> A unified digital platform integrating **students, faculty, institutions, universities, and government bodies** into a single intelligent education ecosystem.

---

### 💡 Why This Project Matters

- Addresses **fragmented education data** at a national scale  
- Designed for **real-world government adoption**  
- Aligns with **Digital India** and **NEP 2020**  
- Enables **data-driven governance & policy decisions**

---

### 🚀 Key Features & Impact

- 🔹 Centralized academic data warehouse  
- 🔹 Role-based dashboards (Student, Faculty, Admin, Govt)  
- 🔹 AI-powered analytics & recommendations  
- 🔹 API-based integration with **AISHE, NIRF, NAAC**  
- 🔹 Secure, scalable, compliance-ready architecture  

---

### 🧰 Tech Stack

**Frontend:** React.js, HTML5, CSS3  
**Backend:** REST APIs  
**AI / ML:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn  
**Database:** MySQL, MongoDB  
**Architecture:** Centralized Data Warehouse  
**Integrations:** AISHE, NIRF, NAAC (API-based)
